Accabox

For Android and iPhone · free · updates itself

Android

Download the Android app

You're inside the Accabox app, so the download opens in Chrome.

  1. Installed the app before 1 November? Uninstall it first, one time only: press and hold its icon, then tap Uninstall. Your account, bets and friends are safe; you just sign in again. From version 1.3 on, updates install straight over the top.
  2. Tap Download the Android app. If Chrome asks whether to keep the file, tap Download anyway.
  3. Open the downloaded file from the notification or your Files app.
  4. If asked, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then go back.
  5. Tap Install. If Play Protect warns about an unknown developer, tap More details then Install anyway.

Not working? Open this page in Chrome rather than inside another app. Or skip the download: open the site in Chrome, tap ⋮ and choose Add to Home screen.

iPhone

Open Accabox in Safari
  1. Open this page in Safari. If you came from WhatsApp, Instagram or Snapchat, tap the menu and choose Open in Safari first.
  2. Tap Open Accabox in Safari above.
  3. Tap the Share button . On newer iPhones, tap ••• at the bottom first, then Share.
  4. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. Leave Open as Web App switched on, then tap Add.
  5. Open Accabox from your home screen and sign in there. It opens full screen like a normal app.

Nothing to download from the App Store. It updates itself, just like the Android app.
